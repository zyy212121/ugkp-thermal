The old transient binary rejects mixture+particles, so timing compares different
application drivers executing the same physical stepping. The steady driver also
collects statistics. Do not describe this as an old/new transient speedup.

Supported addition: frozen multi-species gas + inert particles on the existing
fixed-mesh gasUGKP path, including drag, heat, porosity/species scaling and
ordinary transient species output/restart. Reacting mixture+particles, mixture
scheduled-pressure boundaries, ALE particles and mixture particle boundary-layer
closure retain their existing unsupported contracts. No CHT/CHMT capability is
advertised by the new gas application capability flag.
