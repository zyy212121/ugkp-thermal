Resolved finding
[P2, FIXED] Decreasing positive transport coefficients generated NaN stress work.
Original defect location: common/gasWall/WallLayerMath.H:30 (with the original dependent reconstruction at lines 32-34); corrected implementation now spans lines 26-66.
The expression log1p((muB-muA)/muA) becomes log1p(-1) when muB/muA is smaller than floating-point relative resolution, although both endpoints are strictly positive and finite. Then expm1 gives -1, muA*(1+relative) is zero, and the similarly reconstructed mobility becomes zero. The quadrature evaluates 0/0. This NaN enters LayerFlux::stressWork and the energy residual in common/gasWall/ReactingWallLayer.H:232, including when it is multiplied by zero shear.
Independent temporary probe against the actual production helper:
  thermalWorkVelocityWeight(1e20, 1., 1e20, 1.) -> -nan
The exact result is 0.5 because B is proportional to mu, a case explicitly covered by the helper comment. Archived probe: review-probes/thermal-weight-red.cpp and thermal-weight-red.observed.log (original assertion failed, SIGABRT). No repository files were changed for this probe.
Implemented fix independently inspected: orient the resistance coordinate so mu increases, map the result back with 1-w on reversal, interpolate B with positive endpoint weights, normalize the ordinary weights, and use log weights only when the coefficient ratio or weight range needs them. This addresses both the original log1p(-1) path and the dependent interpolation/weight overflow issues. The actual production-helper test covers 1e20, 1e100, and 1e300 ratios in both directions, plus 1e-300 to 1e300 ratios in both directions. All values are finite, the B-proportional-to-mu result is 0.5, and forward+reverse weights sum to 1.
