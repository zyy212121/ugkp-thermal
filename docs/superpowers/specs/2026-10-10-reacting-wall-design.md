# Reacting boundary-layer implementation contract

## Scope
Implement a shared auxiliary reacting-wall family in gasUGKP and CHMT, retaining the existing low-Re and ordinary wall-function families. This document defines the technical interface, inventory ownership, numerical scheme and verification requirements.

## Public core contract
All core types and functions live in namespace ugkwp::gaswall, under common/gasWall/. They are host/device compatible and independent of OpenFOAM, CHMT and allocation.
- WallFamily: LowRe=0, WallFunction=1, BoundaryLayer=2.
- BoundaryLayerModel: ConstantTransport=0, ReactingSst=1, FiniteRate=ReactingSst. The constant model is an explicitly frozen constant-property limit; ReactingSst solves coupled velocity, temperature, composition and optional SST. Zero-blowing and pure thermal-blowing limits are analytic; finite-blowing viscous heating uses a 64-panel Simpson Green-function integral.
- WallModelConfig<Real>: family, model, nodes, maxIterations, relativeTolerance, absoluteTolerance, stretch, turbulentPrandtl, turbulentSchmidt, enableSst.
- WallInput<Real,Ns>: pressure, temperature (Tw), massFlux[Ns] (physical total species wall flux into gas, kg/m2/s), velocity[3] (absolute surface velocity), normal[3] (material toward gas), matchingDistance, ownerDistance; matching state with temperature, velocity[3], massFraction[Ns], k, omega; model with common thermo/mechanism and molecular transport; owner quadrature distances and positive volume weights supplied separately.
- WallOutput<Real,Ns>: traceTemperature, traceDensity, traceMassFraction[Ns], traceVelocity[3], conductiveHeatFlux (into gas), traction[3] (viscous traction on the material-normal plane), wallSpeciesFlux[Ns], matchingSpeciesFlux[Ns], reactionIntegral[Ns] per wall area, chemistryMismatch[Ns], chemistryMismatchAvailable, integratedKSource, ownerOmega, wallKFlux, iterations, residual.
- evaluateWallModel(input, config, workspace, output, status) returns bool; output remains unchanged on failure. Workspace is caller-owned and reusable; no hidden gas inventory. A separate finite-rate branch must not silently become frozen chemistry.
- Owner quadrature is a borrowed view of distances and positive physical volume weights with volume and first moment, geometry version and stage identity. Invalid volume, nodes outside owner or matching domain, unsupported multiple walls or uncertified geometry reject before publishing.
- Transport properties use the same constant mu and species D input available to the current outer solver. Molecular conductivity is either the explicitly specified constant lambda or mu*Cp(local T,Y)/molecularPrandtl, matching the outer transport selection; rho, Cp, h and reactions vary with local state. This is not a new variable-viscosity law.

## Differential equations and energy
Use a wall-attached normal coordinate y, total mass flux m=sum Js, ideal-mixture EOS at matching pressure, absolute tangential velocity and physical normal velocity wallNormalVelocity+m/rho. Solve steady flux balances:
d(m u_t - mu_eff u_t')/dy = 0;
d(m Yi + ji)/dy = wi;
d(sum hi*(m Yi+ji) + m*|u|^2/2 - lambda_eff*T' - tau_t dot u_t)/dy = 0.
Corrected differential diffusion has sum ji=0. Formation-inclusive enthalpy belongs to the common thermodynamics. There is no separate reaction-heat source in the total-energy equation.
The outer gas remains the only owner of mass, species, momentum and total-energy inventories; its chemistry half steps are unchanged. The integrated auxiliary chemistry source is diagnostic only, never a material exchange or extra gas source. chemistryMismatch is the auxiliary integral minus an explicitly supplied coarse integral on the same domain and time. Without that input, chemistryMismatchAvailable=false and each mismatch is NaN. speciesBalanceResidual separately measures matching flux minus physical wall flux minus auxiliary integral.
The physical wall species flux is the specified boundary flux even though matching species flux generally differs. At the wall, sum hi*Js is used exactly once; do not add diffusive species enthalpy again. Pressure/sweep work and absolute kinetic/viscous work are paired by application adapters.

## Numerical strategy
A stretched 1D nodal boundary-value discretization carries two wall-relative tangential velocities, theta=T-Tw, Ns-1 independent mass fractions, sqrt(k) and z=1/sqrt(omega). Nodes are y_i=L*(i/(n-1))^stretch; default n=24 and runtime adapters support up to 128, with bounded scratch capacity buckets. Species, total energy and k use conservative nearest-neighbor face flux differences and common sources. Every shared omega face uses the same endpoint state and two-point SST gradients. Interior source gradients use the quadratic-exact nonuniform three-point derivative.

At nodes i>=2, omega uses its physical conservative flux residual multiplied by -z_i^4/(2*controlVolume*rhoScale). Positive fixed-geometry fitting resolves the smooth-wall omega=A/y^2 asymptote: the diffusive secant omega gradient on each positive-y face is multiplied by (y_left*y_right/y_mid^2)^2; the destruction rho*beta*omega^2 at a node is multiplied by y_i^4*(faceLeft^-3-faceRight^-3)/(3*controlVolume). Production and cross diffusion retain the common SST algebra. This is asymptotic-preserving and second-order consistent at fixed positive y, but not a linear-exact omega gradient at finite mesh width. For finite wall omega, fixed-index near-wall pointwise errors need not vanish; the affected region shrinks with refinement, so finite-positive-blowing integral/output refinement is required separately.

Cross diffusion differentiates physical omega directly wherever both endpoints have finite omega. Applying a discrete chain rule to z instead creates a spurious sharp matching-end omega peak. Only the singular first interval uses the analytic relation omega'=-2*z'/z^3.

Zero blowing: z(0)=0 and k(0)=0. The first positive node imposes z_1=z_0+y_1*sqrt(rho_wall*beta1/(6*mu)). This is a leading near-wall boundary-location approximation; it is not an additional exact differential-equation row. Positive blowing uses z_0=sqrt(nu_wall/uTau^2 * vPlus*(1+5*vPlus)/25), Wilcox 2008 equations (17)-(18), where vPlus=(m/rho_wall)/uTau and uTau=sqrt(abs(tau_wall)/rho_wall). vPlus is dimensionless; omega has units 1/s, z has units sqrt(s). The positive-blowing expression tends continuously to z_0=0. No nonzero wall shear means this positive-blowing branch is inapplicable.

Below y_1, physical k is reconstructed continuously through both blowing limits. Let a=sqrt(rho_wall*beta1/(6*mu)), ell=z_0/a, p=(1+sqrt(1+24*betaStar/beta1))/2 and g(y)=(y+ell)^p*[1-(ell/(y+ell))^(2*p-1)]. Use k(y)=k_1*g(y)/g(y_1)*exp[m*(y-y_1)/(2*mu)], with the ell=0 limit k_1*(y/y_1)^p. This avoids the incorrect k~y^2 induced by linear sqrt(k) interpolation. The finite-ell near-wall limit is k~y and the wall k flux is -mu*k'(0) from this same reconstruction. The exponential removes the leading convection term; the omitted local potential is O(Pe_1^2), Pe_1=m*y_1/mu. It remains a leading asymptotic approximation, requiring first-cell refinement at appreciable Pe_1. Quadrature uses this physical k and its derivative below y_1. At and above y_1 it positively interpolates the full nodal sourceK already evaluated with the BVP centered gradients, rather than recomputing a nearly cancelling production/destruction difference using different interval gradients. This is a source-field reconstruction, not an exact pointwise constitutive evaluation. Its geometry quadrature and first-cell asymptotic error need separate refinement. Owner omega is sampled at finite owner distance.

A positive diffusion/destruction Picard iteration supplies an initial guess only (at most 200 sweeps, 0.5 relaxation). Its upwind convection never authorizes the final result: the full coupled residual must converge independently. Damped Newton uses a block-tridiagonal finite-difference Jacobian and physical-feasibility line search. Species derivatives use a local dominant-species tangent basis, avoiding an infeasible direction at double-zero simplex corners while recovering the original global independent-coordinate Jacobian. Only O(machine epsilon) negative Newton motion from an exactly zero species is removed as linear-elimination roundoff; trial states are never broadly clipped or chemistry-frozen. Thermodynamic temperatures must remain within every species table's valid interval.

Internal thermal and mechanical conditioning preserves the physical equations: tangential velocity is relative to the wall and temperature is theta=T-Tw. A single constant Href=sum(Y_match,s*h_s(Tw)) is subtracted from species energy fluxes. Each species retains h_s(Tw)-Href plus its Cp integral from Tw to T, so formation-enthalpy differences and differential-diffusion energy remain present. Linear-Cp increments are analytic; NASA7 increments use exact three-point integration of the quartic Cp polynomial, split across the table breakpoint and retaining its tabulated enthalpy offset. Only the cancellation-prone breakpoint offset is accumulated in at least double precision, once per species per solve; all FP32 residuals and matrices remain FP32. The rounded physical temperature selects the same thermodynamic branch as the common Cp/EOS evaluation.

The internal energy flux equals physical energy minus m*Href minus wallTangentialVelocity dot physicalMomentumFlux plus m*|wallTangentialVelocity|^2/2. This constant linear combination of conservative equations changes neither the converged physical energy nor wall heat. Physical normal velocity remains absolute. Public outputs remain physical; reactingWallProfileTemperature(input,workspace,node) restores Tw+theta, while detail::physicalLayerEnergyFlux restores formation-inclusive absolute-frame energy for diagnostics. Normal-velocity gradients use a product-difference form that avoids subtracting nearly equal absolute states.

FP64 Jacobian columns retain one-sided sqrt(epsilon) differences. FP32 non-species columns use feasible central differences with cube-root-epsilon local-gradient scales, falling back to a feasible one-sided direction at a boundary. Temperature uses the compensated theta scale. Every derivative is normalized by its actual representable perturbation, and simplex donor corrections use the actual donor-to-target perturbation ratio. FP32 adds at most five reverse perturbations per node (Ns=2: 6 to 11; Ns=10: 14 to 19), with corresponding additional local residual and chemistry-rate evaluations, but no additional whole-domain or geometry traversal. Context grows by two Ns-sized enthalpy caches and one scalar reference; the persistent scratch ABI is unchanged. Common SST algebra uses GpuReal, so SST calls with another scalar type explicitly return Unsupported; laminar generic types remain available. Failed iteration, singular Jacobian or inadmissible trial never publishes a partial output. WallStatus carries the actual latest nonlinear residual (NaN if none exists), iteration and code. NumericalNonConvergence is not reclassified as a physically unsupported input.

Device compilation keeps five repeated thermochemistry/SST residual leaves out of line to bound capacity-specialized compiler growth; host calls remain inline. This does not change equations or introduce recursion, and compilation-resource measurements do not establish GPU runtime speed or stack safety.

The residual, source balance and mesh refinement are distinct tests. Caller-owned scratch has no heap allocation or hidden inventory. Convergence is not an engineering validation claim.

### Precision and current measured limits
Host tests instantiate R=GpuReal in separate FP32 and FP64 executables; this is generic-core precision evidence, not a native FP32 backend or GPU-runtime claim. Current gasUGKP and CHMT production wall state is double. Default relativeTolerance remains 1e-8, and explicit strict tolerances are never clamped. FP32 callers in the tested range explicitly configure .01; strict 1e-8 requests still report nonconvergence rather than silently loosening the criterion.

For the SST k_match=.5, omega_match=200, U_match-U_wall=30 case, all 20 combinations of N=24/48/96/128 and mass flux=0/1e-9/.001/.01/.1 converge in both precision builds. FP32 uses 4-9 Newton iterations and reaches maximum residual .009565924; FP64 uses 5-10 iterations at tolerance 1e-8. Maximum FP32-versus-FP64 relative errors are .00699% in shear, .00514% in heat and .000402% in owner omega, below the predeclared .5% precision-comparison threshold. A tangential Galilean shift from wall/matching velocities 0/30 to 1000/1030 produces bitwise-identical physical heat, shear and omega in each tested precision. These comparisons isolate arithmetic precision; they do not establish mesh-converged physical accuracy.

Both precisions also exercise ten-species strong reaction with eight initially exact-zero inert species, new-product creation, an exhausted global dependent species and the double-zero simplex corner. FP32 inert drift after the strong solve is at most 5.47e-9 in mass fraction (below one machine epsilon), rather than falsely requiring double-precision roundoff. A simultaneous exothermic differential-diffusion reaction/SST/nonzero-blowing smoke test checks finite outputs, species balance and formation-inclusive energy conservation. The more stringent FP64 assertions remain separate from FP32 arithmetic bounds.

After the physical-omega cross-diffusion correction, zero-blowing N48/96/128 shear is 1.02670/1.03682/1.03675 and heat flux -17.0349/-17.2462/-17.2512 for the same case. Finite m=.001 gives shear 1.02817/1.03688/1.03524. Stronger m=.1 remains underresolved in shear and reconstructed wall k flux at the production maximum; this is an accuracy gap, not a physical-input rejection. Owner source integration must also be independently quadrature-refined, especially when positive production and negative destruction nearly cancel. No single two-point quadrature or converged nonlinear residual establishes that integral's accuracy.

## SST inventory replacement
Use the consistently reconstructed full k-source field at supplied physical owner-volume quadrature points; the existing nodal evaluation traversal also fills this source cache. Replace the existing full owner k source exactly once; do not add it to the old source or overwrite the k inventory. Apply one physical wall k flux. The omega owner value is sampled at finite ownerDistance; replace its old update row and projection and account its actual inventory change in the constraint budget. Do not volume-average singular omega through y=0. Keep source/transport/constraint channels for k and omega.

## Adapter and geometry obligations
Keep lowRe and ordinary wallFunction behavior unchanged. Ordinary wallFunction is valid only when every physical transfer channel is zero. BoundaryLayer requires zero gas contact resistance and matched CPU/GPU inputs, including the candidate Tw and species wall fluxes. The CPU material iteration and GPU gas stage call the same core.
Each supported owner has one physical wall, planar support face, certified star-shaped volume, positive owner-volume quadrature and a gas-connected matching ray extending beyond the complete owner projection. Match using local positive weights on unconstrained gas cells; multiwall corners, warped walls and cyclic constrained stencils reject. Rebuild after geometry changes. A geometric certificate alone cannot replace actual quadrature moment checks. For SST sources, cache a profile-aligned positive section quadrature using the same profile node count/stretch and actual matching distance. Cuts include tetrahedral projection vertices and every profile node; two-point Gauss integrates the quadratic section area times linearly reconstructed source above the first interval. The first interval uses positive Gauss quadrature in sqrt(y/y_1), default order 8, with orders 4 and 16 as refinement controls. This preprocessing is bounded and occurs only when geometry or profile configuration changes. A real cube regression compares N48/96/128 source integrals with independent per-interval Gauss32 and verifies volume/first moment. It removes quadrature aliasing, but the profile's finite-grid source-versus-boundary-flux discrepancy remains separately reported.
Only sparse active-wall faces need profile solves. Reuse existing face/cell traversals for consumption. Geometry rebuild occurs only on geometry change; profile recomputation occurs for changed physical stage inputs. No unconditional whole-domain preflight is introduced.

## Verification and risks
Core: zero/finite blowing analytic limits; common-thermo reacting diffusion against independent reference; meaningful chemistry versus frozen control; formation-enthalpy closure; coordinate rotation; distinct species diffusivities; positivity, invalid inputs, atomic failure; SST smooth-zero continuity and positive-blowing solution; nodal refinement and actual-volume quadrature.
Integration: CPU/GPU Tw semantics, physical versus matching flux distinction, one chemistry owner, single k-source replacement, omega six-channel audit, rejected-trial replay and restart.
Physical two-layer validation still requires wall-resolved same-model mesh/time convergence, 3D inflow/outflow blowing flat plate, dry nonporous recession and nonporous averaged-film evaporation, then reacting counterparts and accepted nonuniform 3D moving geometry. No fitted per-material constants.
Unresolved or failed physical checks remain explicitly outside validated scope. No CUDA runtime claim without native evidence. Current known restrictions (topology changes, wet porous transfer, surface reaction under wet film, resolved-normal film coupling) remain explicit.
