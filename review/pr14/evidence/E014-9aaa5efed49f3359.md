## Availability and direct-impact review

The new publication facts used by this chain are appropriately distinct from status:

- `evaluateSpecies` resets its optional output fact before work and sets it only after assigning the full local bundle.
- `evaluateMixtureEnergy` requires actual species publication even when status is InvalidModel, retains the original species sum order, and publishes its own fact only after both energy/capacity stores. A later unavailable species cannot expose a partial accumulated result.
- Known mixture completion passes that real publication fact through inversion. The inversion temperature fact is set only when the returned temperature is an actual endpoint/saved/trial evaluation selected by the existing algorithm or its terminal completion.
- Recovery uses that fact to distinguish completed metadata failures from missing backing; it does not equate every InvalidModel with a usable output or every InvalidModel with missing data.
- The internal `evaluateCaloricEnergy` callback fact retains its narrower meaning that the callback returned Success. Strict inversion passes nullptr there; strict local values that were not published cannot become a recovery temperature fact. No problematic direct consumer of that internal diagnostic flag was found.

Independent adversarial coverage included null species, null coefficients, missing masses, a second species with an unknown model after an earlier valid species, finite and nonfinite inversion targets, pre-set true availability flags that must be reset, and strict inversion. The unavailable cases keep all relevant old output/seed values and availability false; completed metadata cases publish the actual energy/capacity and selected T with availability true. Missing backing/model recovery retains all eight old fields and a failure status.
