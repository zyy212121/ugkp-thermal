Actual species/coefficient backing remains required. `trace(..., terminalProfile)` writes the original T/rho/Y/U and physical species flux expressions. `wallProperty<Enthalpy>` retains the original finite scalar result, and completes only the wall's required Cp or h when the scalar is unavailable numerically; generic/scalar/material partial-property behavior is unchanged.

- `ReactingWallLayer.H`: final publication completes actual pointState, rates, corrected diffusion, flux, trace and optional source integration. Strict seed/Picard, 200-iteration seed budget/threshold, failed-seed restoration, each two-attempt derivative, Newton update, 40 alpha-halving descent search, original successful fallback and final residual measurement remain.
