## R1: unused face storage was a real regression

V1 incorrectly checked species flux and faceStatus before the original zero-trip, invalid-face and ownership skips. Its blanket claim that those were only consumed-backing checks was false. The independent 30-case probe demonstrated original successful status0 calls becoming InvalidStorage, including six empty moving-volume cases losing their real density/energy/species halving. The failed V1 evidence and claim remain preserved, not rewritten as correct.

The corrected order is precise:

1. Keep the original thread/cell and strict prior-cell-status returns.
2. Follow the original cell incidence loop, invalid-face skip and actual ownership skip.
3. Check faceStatus backing immediately before its real read.
4. Follow the original strict face-status return; only then check flux backing immediately before its species loop.
5. Check inventory rho immediately before its actual final species inventory read, after earlier original numerical strict returns.

Thus absent face arrays are harmless when never consumed. A strict prior-face-status return also does not inspect later unused flux or inventory rho. Actual consumed missing backing still reports InvalidStorage. No face prescan, count-based admission rule, status whitelist, replacement value or added traversal was introduced. Bulk/species FP64 expressions, closure/carrier repair, face/species order, moving volume algebra and once-only budget increments are untouched.
