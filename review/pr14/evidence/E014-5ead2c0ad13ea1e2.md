## CW-1: inverse bracket can loop forever for newly admitted numerical parameters

`common/wall/GpuColdWallSolidification.H:182–190` retains an unbounded bracket-growth loop. Take `meltingTemperatureK = -INFINITY`, finite positive mushy range and solid cp (20 and 1273), finite latent heat (1.16e6), and a finite stored enthalpy such as 1. The solidus, liquidus, both phase-boundary enthalpies, and initial upper bound are negative infinity. The two inverse early branches are false. `coldWallSpecificEnthalpyJkg(upper)` takes its solid branch and returns negative infinity, so both growth predicates remain true. Multiplying upper by 1.5 never changes it. Consequently host and FSH profile routes do not reach profile, phase/history, temperature, or ledger publication. The former numerical parameter check rejected this input; deleting it newly exposes the loop through these owners.

This finding is proved by following the source's IEEE branches; no CPU or GPU numerical test was run. The repair must make nonprogressing/nonfinite bracket growth reach the bounded native inverse arithmetic and actual publication. It must not introduce rejection, fictitious results, or change ordinary successful bracket growth and forty-bisection ordering. CHT's paired inverse uses a separately bounded `ParticleCaloric::temperature`; this specific loop finding concerns the host/FSH inverse.
