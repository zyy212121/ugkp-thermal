The bounded fixture has real allocated arrays, rho=1e307, p=1 and finite formation coefficient 1; the mass-weighted CV overflows while selected energy and separately normalized CV remain available. No out-of-bounds test or missing storage is involved.

Evidence: `logs/e-only-red.log`, `e-only-red-temp/test_original_energy_only_capa0/{original,current}.cpp`, compiler logs, executables and outputs. g++ -O2 -ffp-contract=off.
